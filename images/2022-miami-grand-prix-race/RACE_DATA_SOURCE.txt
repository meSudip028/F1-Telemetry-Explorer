2022 race analysis data source
Source: Jolpica-F1 public timing/classification API.

These charts use lap times and recorded race positions. They do not contain car telemetry channels such as speed, throttle, brake, gear, or GPS position.
Position progression uses reported position by lap; lap-time progression includes recorded times, so pit stops, traffic and safety-car periods can create spikes.
The fastest recorded lap comparison is computed from the available lap-timing feed and may not match official classification in all edge cases.
